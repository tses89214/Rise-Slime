    How to play

    Slimes work at the stations. Tap + on a station to send a slime over from the Lounge, − to send one back. Buy Slime brings new ones to the Lounge. Upgrade makes a station faster.

    The chain

    • Farm: each slime makes 1 wheat every 1s.
    • Mill: each slime turns 2 wheat into 1 flour every 3s.
    • Bakery: each slime turns 2 flour into 1 bread every 5s.
    • Shop: every bread sells for 10 coins.

    A slime only starts a job when its ingredients are on hand, so each station needs the one before it to keep up. Tip: a 4 : 6 : 5 split keeps all three busy.

    Upgrades

    Each level makes a station 10% faster. Levels 10, 25 and 50 double its speed on top of that.

    Queue bar

    It shows how much of the previous product is waiting at a station (the ticks mark , and ).

    • Starved: nothing to work on. The slimes sleep.
    • Fine: under waiting.
    • Getting busy: or more.
    • Backing up: or more. Add workers or upgrade.
    • Jammed: or more.
